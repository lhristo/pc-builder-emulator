# Privacy Policy

PC Builder does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

The app stores saved PC builds locally on the user's device or browser using local storage. Saved builds are not uploaded to a server by this app.

The app does not use analytics, advertising trackers, location services, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

Component names, prices, and compatibility guidance are included for planning. Users should confirm final specifications and prices with the seller or manufacturer before buying parts.

For App Store privacy questions, the expected answer is that this app does not collect data.
